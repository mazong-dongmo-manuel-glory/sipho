Sipho sound effects
===================

All ten effects are derived from sound packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). Credit is not required;
it is given here as a courtesy and for our licence records.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Packs (downloaded from OpenGameArt.org):
  - Interface Sounds       https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds
  - Music Jingles          https://opengameart.org/content/85-short-music-jingles
  - RPG Sound Effects      https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects

The files were converted to mono OGG Vorbis, trimmed, faded out and
loudness-matched with app/tool/build_sounds.py (re-run it to regenerate them).

To replace a sound with a final recording, drop a file with the same name
(e.g. level_up.ogg) in this folder: no code change is needed.
